**Truncation semantics** (new behavior, no old version to diff against, so I picked these boundary rules and tested them):
- `max_length=None` (default): unchanged, no truncation.
- Slug already `<= max_length`: returned as-is.
- Otherwise, slice to `max_length`, then look for the rightmost hyphen inside that slice and cut there — this naturally also removes any trailing hyphen left exactly at the boundary, so no separate `.rstrip("-")` step was needed.
- No hyphen available in the window (single long word): falls back to a hard cut at `max_length`.
- `max_length <= 0`: clamped to `0`, returning `""`.
