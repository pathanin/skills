# Translate to Thai

Translate into natural Thai. Keep technical terms, product names, and specific keywords in English wherever that reads more naturally to a Thai speaker — don't force a Thai word just because one exists.

Match the author's tone and register. English terms sit inside Thai grammar, not the reverse.

Output the translation only — no preamble or explanation.
